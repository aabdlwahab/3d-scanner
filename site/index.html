Questions

Why isn’t this in the App Store?
ScanSpace is installed straight from this page. iOS still requires every app to be signed by an Apple account: the one-tap button uses the owner’s developer signature (your iPhone must be registered for it), while SideStore and AltStore sign the app with your own free Apple ID.
“Unable to install” or “integrity could not be verified”
The one-tap build only installs on iPhones whose UDID is in the signing profile. Ask the owner to add your device, or use the SideStore / AltStore option instead.
“Developer Mode required”
Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode, switch it on and restart when asked. It’s needed for apps signed with a free Apple ID.
The app stopped opening after a week
Apps signed with a free Apple ID expire after 7 days. Open SideStore or AltStore and tap Refresh (AltStore can refresh automatically when AltServer runs on your computer on the same Wi-Fi). Your scans stay on the phone.
Which iPhones work?
Any iPhone or iPad with a LiDAR Scanner on iOS 17 or later: iPhone 12 Pro / Pro Max and every newer Pro model (including the iPhone 15 Pro), and iPad Pro 2020 or newer.
Is my data uploaded anywhere?
No. Scanning, processing and exporting all happen on your iPhone. Nothing leaves it unless you share an export.
